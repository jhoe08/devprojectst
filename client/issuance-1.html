SUBJECT: Amended Guidelines on Hybrid Work Arrangements

1.0 Purpose and Rationale

This Circular updates institutional guidelines concerning remote and on-site operational hours. The amendments aim to maintain operational continuity, enhance collaborative output, and sustain consistency with our established corporate productivity benchmarks.

2.0 Scope and Coverage

This issuance applies strictly to all full-time regular employees, probationary staff, and contractual personnel operating within the corporate headquarters and secondary regional satellite branches.

3.0 Core Policy Directives

  • 3.1Mandatory Core On-Site Days: All operational teams must report physically to their designated workspaces on Mondays and Wednesdays.
  • 3.2Core Collaborative Hours: Inter-departmental meetings, syncs, and operational alignments must be scheduled strictly between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

4.0 Compliance and Monitoring

Failure to adhere to these standardized operational requirements will trigger an immediate review under Article IV (Code of Discipline) of the Employee Handbook. Division Chiefs are directed to submit weekly logs.

5.0 Effectivity

This Order shall take effect immediately upon digital distribution and official publication on the institutional intranet portal.
